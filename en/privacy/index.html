Privacy Policy

How we use and store submissions and resumes, and handle privacy requests.

Policy version: privacy-v1.0-2026-10

1. Information we collect

Talent: basic profile, nationality, residence and immigration information; education, experience and language skills; career and work preferences; email and contact details; and an optional resume.

Companies: company, contact person and contact details; hiring roles, headcount and timing; education, experience and language requirements; and foreign hiring information.

We also process consent choices and times, the policy version, submission language and start time, and anti-spam information.

2. How we use information

We use talent profiles, resumes and company inquiries for service review and contact. With optional consent, we review suitable Korean company opportunities internally and contact the applicant first when needed.

3. Retention

Submitted information and resumes may be kept for service review and contact for up to one year from collection. When the purpose is fulfilled or deletion is requested, we delete the information without delay after any necessary verification.

4. Storing resumes and submissions

We store operating information in Google Sheets and submitted resumes in a private Google Drive folder. We do not make resume files publicly shared and limit access to staff who need it for their work.

5. Opportunity review

Consent to opportunity review is optional. You can submit a profile without it. This consent does not automatically share your resume or personal information with a specific company. If sharing with a company is needed, we proceed after confirming with you.

6. Correction and deletion requests

You may request correction or deletion of your submitted information. After receiving a request, we make the correction or delete the information without delay following necessary identity checks. See the contact section below for how to make a request.

7. External services

Google Apps Script receives submissions, and Google Sheets and Google Drive store them. Staff receive email notifications of new submissions. OpenAI and Gemini are not used in the current form submission process.

8. Contact

For privacy inquiries or requests to access, correct or delete your personal information, please email shim8568@gmail.com.
