About Korea Talent Gate

A platform built to help global talent and Korean companies explore better possibilities together.

Why Korea Talent Gate

Finding work and hiring across borders can be complex. Talent needs clear information about careers and preparation, while companies need a practical way to explore global hiring possibilities. Korea Talent Gate brings these two starting points together.

What We Do

For Global Talent

Help talent organize their background, career goals and preparation for opportunities in Korea.

For Korean Companies

Help companies clarify hiring needs and explore global talent possibilities.

Practical Guidance

Provide practical information on work preparation, workplace culture, stay and administrative procedures.

How We Work

01Understand

Start with the actual goals and conditions of both talent and companies.

02Review

Review background, requirements and practical considerations.

03Move Forward

When there is a meaningful fit, the next steps can be considered.

What We Are Building

Korea Talent Gate is growing as a platform connecting global talent, Korean companies and information about working in Korea. We continue to expand our features and information based on the needs of talent and companies.
