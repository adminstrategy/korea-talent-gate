About Korea Talent Gate

A platform that helps global talent and Korean companies understand each other's needs and explore new opportunities.

Why Korea Talent Gate

Finding work and hiring across borders can be complex. Talent needs clear information about careers and preparation, while companies need a practical way to understand global hiring requirements. Korea Talent Gate brings these two starting points together.

What We Do

For Global Talent

Help talent organize their background, career goals and preparation for opportunities in Korea.

For Korean Companies

Help companies clarify hiring needs and review potential matches with global talent.

Practical Guidance

Provide practical information on work preparation, workplace culture, stay and administrative procedures.

How We Work

01Understand

Start with the actual goals and conditions of both talent and companies.

02Review

Review background, requirements and practical considerations.

03Move Forward

When talent and company requirements align, we consider the next steps.

What We Are Building

Korea Talent Gate is growing as a platform connecting global talent, Korean companies and information about working in Korea. We continue to expand our features and information based on the needs of talent and companies.
