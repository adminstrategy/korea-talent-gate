Preparing for a Career in Korea

Organize your career goals, background, language skills and materials for opportunities in Korea.

Key Points

Define your target role

Identify the roles you are interested in and the work you want to do.

Connect your background

Consider how your education, major and experience relate to your target role.

Prepare for workplace languages

Identify the Korean, English or other language skills the role may require.

Organize career materials

Present your responsibilities, skills and experience clearly in your resume and supporting materials.

Preparation Checklist

  • Desired role
  • Highest education & major
  • Relevant experience
  • Language levels
  • Available start date
