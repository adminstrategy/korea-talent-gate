Understanding Korean Workplaces

Prepare for workplace communication, collaboration, meetings and everyday professional expectations.

Key Points

Workplace communication

Check the languages, communication channels and practices used by your team.

Reporting and collaboration

Understand how progress is shared and how responsibilities are coordinated.

Meetings and schedules

Clarify expectations for meeting preparation, shared schedules and punctuality.

Etiquette and expectations

Learn how colleagues address one another, show respect and discuss work expectations.

Workplace culture can vary by company and industry.

Preparation Checklist

  • Team languages & communication channels
  • Progress updates & collaboration
  • Meetings & schedule management
  • Everyday etiquette & expectations
